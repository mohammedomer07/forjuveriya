Celebrating Your
Beautiful Existence
Dear, Juveriya 🤍
- Mohammed Omer
* August 05, 2006 (Saturday) — the day Allah ﷻ blessed this world by entrusting your parents with a beautiful amanah.

* Today, it has been 7,305 days; 175,320 hours; 10,519,200 minutes; 1,152,000 seconds since that precious blessing first smiled upon this world.

* With every passing day, Allah ﷻ has written another page of your story with wisdom, mercy, and countless unseen blessings.
* You are like a flower lovingly nurtured by its Creator—still blooming, yet already bringing beauty wherever you go.
* May your heart always remain attached to Allah, your character illuminated by the Sunnah, and your soul comforted by the Qur'an.


* I pray that every step you take becomes a step closer to Jannah, every tear becomes a means of purification, and every smile becomes a source of sadaqah.
* May Allah protect you from every evil eye, every hardship, and every sorrow that was never written to strengthen you.
* May He grant you a life filled with barakah, sincere love, righteous companions, and countless moments of peace.


* If I were asked to describe you, I wouldn't count the stars in the sky—I would count the duas I make for your happiness.
* If memories could be preserved forever, every moment shared with you would deserve eternity.
* And if I had to choose only one wish today, it would be this: May Allah write your name among the people of Jannatul Firdaus, and may your smile continue to brighten the lives of everyone blessed to know you.
* There is so much my heart wishes to tell you today, yet I choose silence where respect is more beautiful than a thousand speeches.
* Some feelings are too sincere to be spoken aloud; they are safer in the hands of Allah ﷻ than on the lips of people.
* Perhaps not every admiration needs to be confessed, and not every emotion needs a voice. Some are meant to remain a silent du'a in the depths of the night.
* If I continued writing everything I wished for you, these pages would never reach an ending.
* So today, I leave the rest unsaid—not because my heart has nothing more to offer, but because Allah ﷻ knows every word I chose to keep within it.
* My greatest wish has never been to be remembered by you; it has always been to know that Allah ﷻ keeps you safe, wherever life leads you.
* May every chapter that Allah ﷻ writes for you be more beautiful than the one before, until the final chapter opens its doors to Jannatul Firdaus.
* And if there is one sentence I choose not to complete today... I trust Allah ﷻ already knows how it ends.
